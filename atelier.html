THE HAND BEHIND THE HANDWORK

A living tradition,
stitched forward.

Poshak brings regional textile traditions into the way we dress today. We look to the colour of a mehndi evening, the drape of a family heirloom, and the patient geometry of hand embroidery.

Our pieces begin with considered fabric and thoughtful tailoring. Artisans then add the finishing touches by hand, so each outfit feels personal—not produced in a hurry.
